# Plan

Write what you want to post, in plain words. One line per post: which media, which networks, when.
Lines starting with > are pasted notes from other people. Signalpost reads them for context but never makes a post from them.

- Example: Teaser reel (media/teaser.mp4): Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts on Friday at 18:00.
- Example: Product photos (media/photo-1.jpg to media/photo-3.jpg): Instagram carousel and Pinterest on Monday morning. Pinterest links to the shop.
- Example: Text announcement for LinkedIn and Threads next Tuesday at 12:00.
